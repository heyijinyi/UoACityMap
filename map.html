About this map unofficial

A student-made, non-commercial wayfinding aid for the University of Auckland City Campus. It is not an official University product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Auckland.

Indoor data (rooms, floors, building outlines) is © University of Auckland. It is not part of this project: your browser fetched it directly from the University's public map service (maps.auckland.ac.nz) and stored it on this device only. Last downloaded: –.

Streets, neighbouring buildings and entrances are © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence.

The 3D shapes, stairs, walls, street levels and room colours are approximations made for orientation. Do not rely on them for safety or accessibility decisions; the official map is the authority.

If you are a rights holder and want something changed or removed, open an issue on this project's GitHub repository and it will be taken down promptly.
